Interests

I am interested in the neural representations underlying complex and continuous cognitive and behavioral tasks in everyday life. My research uses fMRI measurements and encoding and decoding models to investigate how perception, memory, attention, and action selection are integrated in the brain, and how these processes contribute to goals and decision making.
I am also interested in brain AI fusion approaches for understanding naturalistic human cognition.
